# People Don't Want to Be Fixed... They Want to Be Seen & Not Judged

**Applied Behavior Profiling** · an 8-week field course by Curtis A. Arnold

### ▶ [Open the live course](https://applied-behavior-profiling.vercel.app)

Most of us listen to _what_ people say. This course teaches you to hear _why_ they felt the need to say it: the social need they're chasing, how they justify their decisions, and what they value most. The last week turns the lens around, onto your own authority, habits, and limiting beliefs.

It's free, it works on a phone, and there are no accounts. Your progress stays in your own browser.

> These skills are for understanding and helping people, never for manipulating them.

## How the course is built

Five units over eight weeks, about 3–4 hours a week: roughly one hour of short lessons and two to three hours of practice. Every lesson reads in under ten minutes and opens with a story.

### Three ground rules

1. **Read the moment, not the life story.** The same person can show different needs at work, with friends, and at home.
2. **Two is fine.** If you hear two needs, pillars, or values, speak to both.
3. **Listen for why, not what.** Ask why the person felt the need to say it.

## The three frameworks

**Needs** are what someone is chasing from other people. **Decision pillars** are how they choose. **Values** are where they're ultimately headed.

| Six needs    | Six decision pillars | Six values  |
| ------------ | -------------------- | ----------- |
| Significance | Deviance             | Connection  |
| Acceptance   | Novelty              | Information |
| Approval     | Social               | Recognition |
| Intelligence | Conformity           | Experiences |
| Pity         | Investment           | Freedom     |
| Strength     | Necessity            | Growth      |

Week 7 joins them into one working sentence:

> **If I** [decision pillar], **then people will** [meet my need], **so I can finally** [value].

Three things to listen for: **adjectives** reveal values, **justifications** reveal pillars, and **repetitions** reveal needs.

## Practice and assessment

Practice matters more than lessons, and it runs on two tracks each week:

- **Controlled:** watch interviews, podcasts, or TV and log what you hear, with the exact phrase that tipped you off.
- **Live:** short everyday conversations where you identify, test, and respond, then write a two-line debrief.

There's no teacher, so the grade is a private scorecard you keep yourself:

| Component                      | Weight |
| ------------------------------ | ------ |
| Weekly practice logs           | 40%    |
| Unit checks (Units 1–3)        | 20%    |
| Capstone: three field profiles | 30%    |
| Self-development plan          | 10%    |

**The capstone:** profile three people, one from a recorded interview and two from live conversations. For each, give the likely need, pillar, and value with the exact phrase behind each; the profile sentence; and one alternative read you ruled out. A profile passes when the evidence actually supports the read and your response matched the need.
